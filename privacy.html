Privacy Policy

Bunker Shot · Effective October 10, 2026

Bunker Shot ("Bunker Shot," "we," "us") is a group-round golf app. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have. We built Bunker Shot to work with as little personal data as possible, and we never sell your data.

Information we collect

No account. Bunker Shot has no sign-up and no password. We don't ask for your email address, and we don't run servers that store your rounds.

Round and score data. Scores, stats, hole notes, and club distances you enter are stored on your device. If you start a group round or share your profile with friends, that data is stored in your own iCloud account through Apple CloudKit and shared only with the players you invite. We can't read it.

Location. With your permission, Bunker Shot uses your precise location while the app is in use to show GPS yardages and find nearby courses. Your location stays on your device. Searches for nearby courses send an approximate map area to OpenStreetMap, as described below. Location is never sold or shared with advertisers.

Camera. If you use "Scan a Friend's QR Code" to add a friend, the camera is used locally to read the code. No photo or video is stored or transmitted.

Photos. A profile photo you choose is stored on your device and, if you share your profile, in your own iCloud account.

Content you report. If you report a chat message, post, or comment written by another player, a copy of that text, the author's display name, and the app version are sent to us through Apple CloudKit so we can review the report. Your name isn't attached to the report. We keep reports only as long as we need them to handle the report.

Diagnostics (optional, off by default). Settings has a "Share Anonymous Product Analytics" toggle. In the current version, usage events are kept only in a small log on your device and are not sent to us. If that changes, they will be anonymous (screens viewed, feature usage, and performance timings), never your name, precise location, or score notes, and this policy will be updated first.

What we don't do

  • We don't use third-party advertising SDKs or trackers.
  • We don't sell or rent your personal data to anyone.
  • We don't collect data from your device's contacts, photo library, or other apps.

Services we use to run the app

  • Apple CloudKit: stores group rounds, friend profiles, and the social feed in your own iCloud account, and receives content reports. Governed by Apple's privacy practices.
  • Open-Meteo: provides weather forecasts for the course you're viewing. Only course coordinates are sent, no personal data.
  • OpenStreetMap (Overpass API): provides golf course location and layout data. Only the map area being searched is sent, no personal data.
  • WhatsApp (optional): if you choose to invite a friend via WhatsApp, the app opens WhatsApp with the invite text. We don't send your data to WhatsApp ourselves.

Your choices

Delete your data from this device. Settings → "Clear Downloaded Courses & Data" erases saved rounds, scores, notes, social data, and saved partners. Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device.

Delete your iCloud data. Group rounds and your shared profile live in your own iCloud account. Remove them in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Bunker Shot Golf.

Block or report players. Press and hold a message, post, or comment to report it or block its author. Blocked players are listed in Settings → Blocked Players.

Turn off analytics. Settings → "Share Anonymous Product Analytics."

Location and camera access can be changed anytime in iOS Settings → Bunker Shot Golf.

Data retention

Data on your device stays there until you clear it or delete the app. Data in your iCloud account stays until you delete it. Content reports are deleted once they have been reviewed and resolved.

Children's privacy

Bunker Shot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

All network traffic uses encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Data stored in iCloud is protected by Apple's iCloud security.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. Material changes will be noted here.
