Privacy Policy

Bunker Shot · Effective August 16, 2026

Bunker Shot ("Bunker Shot," "we," "us") is a group-round golf app. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have. We built Bunker Shot to work with as little personal data as possible, and we never sell your data.

Information we collect

Account information. If you create an account to sync rounds across devices or play group rounds, we store your email address, display name, and a salted, hashed version of your password — we never store or have access to your plaintext password.

Round and score data. Scores, stats, hole notes, and club distances you enter are stored on your device and, if you're signed in, synced to our servers and/or Apple CloudKit so your rounds stay available across your devices and can be shared with playing partners you invite.

Location. With your permission, Bunker Shot uses your precise location while the app is in use to show GPS yardages and find nearby courses. Location is not collected when the app isn't in use, and it isn't sold or shared with advertisers.

Camera. If you use "Scan QR Code" to add a friend, the camera is used locally to read the code. No photo or video is stored or transmitted.

Photos. A profile photo you choose is stored on your device and, if you're signed in, included in your account backup.

Diagnostics (optional, off by default). Settings has a "Share Anonymous Product Analytics" toggle. When enabled, we receive anonymized product usage events (screens viewed, feature usage) and performance timings — never your name, email, precise location, or score notes. This data is used only to improve the app and is not shared with third-party advertisers.

What we don't do

  • We don't use third-party advertising SDKs or trackers.
  • We don't sell or rent your personal data to anyone.
  • We don't collect data from your device's contacts, photo library, or other apps.

Services we use to run the app

  • Apple CloudKit — syncs group rounds and friend-share links, governed by Apple's privacy practices.
  • Open-Meteo — provides weather forecasts for the course you're viewing; only course coordinates are sent, no personal data.
  • OpenStreetMap (Overpass API) — provides golf course location and layout data; no personal data is sent.
  • WhatsApp (optional) — if you choose to invite a friend via WhatsApp, this opens WhatsApp's own share sheet. We don't send your data to WhatsApp ourselves.

Your choices

Delete your account. Settings → Account & Sync → Delete Account permanently deletes your account and synced rounds from our servers and erases saved rounds, scores, and social data from your device. This can't be undone.

Clear local data without deleting your account. Settings → "Clear Downloaded Courses & Data."

Turn off analytics. Settings → "Share Anonymous Product Analytics."

Location and camera access can be changed anytime in iOS Settings → Bunker Shot.

Data retention

We keep account and round data for as long as your account is active, or until you delete your account. Locally stored data stays on your device until you clear it or delete the app.

Children's privacy

Bunker Shot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Passwords are hashed with a salted PBKDF2-SHA256 derivation before storage — we never see or store a plaintext password.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. Material changes will be noted here.
