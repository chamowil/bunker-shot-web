Account & sync

Create or sign in to an account from Settings → Account & Sync to keep rounds synced across devices and play group rounds. Signing in is optional — the app works fully offline without an account.

To permanently delete your account and synced rounds, go to Settings → Account & Sync → Delete Account.

GPS & location

Bunker Shot needs location access to show yardages and find nearby courses. If yardages seem off, check that location access is set to "While Using the App" in iOS Settings → Bunker Shot, and that you're outdoors with a clear sky view. "Battery Saver GPS Mode" in the app's Settings trades a little accuracy for longer battery life.

Premium & subscriptions

Premium unlocks unlimited offline course downloads, advanced stats, caddie strategy, and group-round tools. Plans include a 14-day free trial, billed monthly or yearly after the trial ends.

Subscriptions are managed entirely through the App Store. To view, change, or cancel your subscription: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. If a purchase didn't unlock Pro, try "Restore Purchases" on the Premium screen.

Adding friends & group rounds

Add a playing partner from Settings → Playing Partners, either by scanning their QR code or sharing an invite link. Group rounds keep everyone's scores in sync in real time.
